Welcome to Bulsho

A community where every learner can thrive.

Bulsho Academy is a friendly, inclusive learning community committed to helping children build strong foundations, discover their strengths, and develop a lifelong love of learning.

Our teachers create engaging classrooms where kindness, curiosity, and effort matter. We work in partnership with families to help each learner feel supported and ready for what comes next.

“We believe a great education begins with belonging—and grows through possibility.”

Our story

2008

Rooted in community. Growing with every generation.

Founded by local educators and families, Bulsho Academy began with a shared hope: to make thoughtful, high-quality learning accessible to children in our community. Today, that founding spirit continues in every classroom.

A tradition of learning and care
